## Platform

web

## Users

Small local business owners and solo operators, starting in the Scenic Rim (Beaudesert, Boonah, Tamborine Mountain, Canungra, Kalbar, Rathdowney and nearby), then Lockyer Valley, Ipswich, Logan and Greater Brisbane. Tradies, cafes, farm stays, shops, makers, clinics. Often time-poor, often older, wary of web people and city agencies, and keen to back a local. They mostly look at the site on a phone, sometimes outdoors.

Their job on this site: decide whether to hand their website over for a free review, and whether the person behind it is local, real and trustworthy.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site for FieldBourne Digital, a one-person local web studio. It exists to get small businesses to request a free website review. After the review, the offer ladder is: a website build plus a monthly care plan, then automation and AI assistants. Success is review requests from real local businesses.

## Positioning

One local person, Beaudesert based and Queensland raised, who reviews your website for free with a plain-English report and a quick mockup, builds the site in your name (domain and DNS always yours), and stays reachable by phone. Not a city agency, not a call centre, not a SaaS product.

## Product Principles

1. Be believed, not impressive. Every claim must be backed by something real.
2. Local and personal over polished and corporate. A face, a town and a phone number beat any trust badge.
3. One clear next step: the free review.
4. Show the work. The concept sites and real review outputs are the proof.
5. Plain words a rural business owner would use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal standard is required. Do sensible accessibility anyway: readable contrast for an older audience on phones outdoors, labelled form fields, and keyboard use.
