## 3. Scenic Rim Regional Council business directory

The council runs a free Business and Community Directory so locals can find and buy local. The online sign-up steps weren't findable, so email the Economic Development team at business@scenicrim.qld.gov.au (or call 07 5540 5111) and ask to be added. Use the short listing above.

"Scenic Rim Business Connect" ($16.99 a month after a trial) is a separate private directory, not the council one. Skip it for now.
