# Outreach templates

Email and LinkedIn templates go here. The `outreach-email` skill (Phase 3) drafts from these.

## Rules every cold email must meet (Spam Act 2003)

- Only send to an address the business published itself.
- The message is relevant to their business.
- It includes Darren's name and ABN.
- It includes a working unsubscribe, and unsubscribes are honoured.
- Log where the address came from and the date it was found. The log lives in `/prospects` (gitignored), not here.

## Channels

- Cold email: yes, under the rules above.
- LinkedIn: manual one-to-one messages only.
- Chambers of commerce and referrals: yes.
- Cold Facebook DMs, automation or scraping: no.

## Unsubscribe line (draft)

> If you'd rather not hear from me again, reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.

Nothing in this folder ever sends anything. Darren sends every email himself.
