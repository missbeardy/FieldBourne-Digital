# Pricing

All prices in AUD. Set in October 2026, deliberately below what most local freelancers charge, because it's a one-person operation with low overheads.

**GST registered:** no

FieldBourne Digital is not registered for GST, so no GST is charged and quotes say so. Registration becomes compulsory once turnover reaches $75,000 a year. When that happens, change this line to "yes" and decide whether prices stay the same (absorbing GST) or go up by 10%.
