# The offer

FieldBourne Digital is a local web studio for small rural and regional businesses, starting in the Scenic Rim.

## Who it's for

- Farm stays and agritourism
- Trades
- Allied health

Scenic Rim first, then Lockyer Valley and Ipswich, then Logan and Greater Brisbane.

## Three steps

1. **Free website review.** A plain look at your current site, with a quick mockup of what it could be. No cost, no catch. This is the main way people start.
2. **A new website, then we look after it.** A site built for how your customers actually find and book you, plus a monthly care plan so it stays working.
3. **Automation and AI assistants.** Take the repetitive admin off your plate: enquiries, bookings, follow-ups, quotes. Built on Make.com by default, Zapier only when an app has no Make support, and custom code only when the job needs it. The FieldBourne CRM and the tradie bundle live here.

## What we always do

- The domain and DNS sit in your name.
- You own your content and photos.
- Prices are shown up front, with GST.
