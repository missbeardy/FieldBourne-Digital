# What each niche's customer is trying to do

Use this to decide what goes on the first screen and in what order. FieldBourne's audience is any small local business or solo operator, so start with the general pattern and use a specific one below only when it fits.

## Any small local business

The customer is usually a local on a phone, often choosing to back a local business over a big one.

- First screen: what you do, the town, and one clear action (call, book, order, visit). On a phone, the call or book button stays on screen.
- Who's behind it: a real name and face. People back people.
- Proof it's real and local: the address or service area, opening hours, real photos, a few real reviews.
- How to get there or get in touch, without hunting.

## Accommodation, farm stays and agritourism

The guest is usually on a phone, often at night, comparing two or three places.

- First screen: a real photo of the place, the town, and **Check dates** or **Book a stay**.
- They want to know fast: what the accommodation is, how many it sleeps, pets, fires, the drive time from Brisbane, and what's nearby (Mt Barney, Lake Moogerah, wineries, the rail trail).
- Show price "from" if the business does, and where bookings happen (their own system, Airbnb, Booking.com).
- Reviews from guests carry a lot of weight. Put them high up.

## Trades

The customer has a problem now and wants someone who will turn up.

- First screen: what you fix, the towns you cover, and a big **Call** button. On a phone the call button stays on screen.
- Licence numbers (QBCC, electrical licence) visible early. They separate a real business from a one-off.
- A short quote form: name, phone, suburb, the job. Photos optional.
- Real job photos labelled by suburb beat any stock image.

## Health and wellness

The patient wants to know if this person can help them, whether they can book online, and what it costs.

- First screen: what you treat, the town, and **Book online** (or **Call to book**).
- Practitioner names, photos and AHPRA registration. Health funds, NDIS, DVA, Medicare care plans if they apply.
- Opening hours and parking. Older patients care about access.
- Be careful with claims. AHPRA advertising rules don't allow testimonials about clinical results, so don't put patient reviews about outcomes in a health mockup.
