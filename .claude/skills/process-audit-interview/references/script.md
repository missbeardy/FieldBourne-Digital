# 45-minute process audit script

A draft for Darren to make his own. Times are a guide. The aim is to listen more than talk.

## 0 to 5 min: Set up

- "Thanks for the time. I'm going to ask how a normal week runs, where things get dropped, and what you'd love to stop doing. I'll take notes. Nothing gets built or charged from today. At the end I'll tell you if there's anything worth doing."
- "Is it OK if I take notes?" (If recording, ask first, and say where the recording goes.)
- What does the business do, who works in it, and what hours?
- Busiest times of year?

## 5 to 15 min: A normal week

- "Walk me through last Monday from when you got up."
- How do new customers find you and get in touch? Phone, email, website form, Facebook, Instagram, booking sites, word of mouth?
- Who answers them, and when? What happens when you're out on a job, with a patient, or out in a paddock with no reception?
- How long does it take to get back to a new enquiry, on a normal day and a busy one?

## 15 to 25 min: The paperwork

Go through each step from first contact to getting paid:

- **Quote or booking.** How do you send it? How long does it take? Do you follow up if they go quiet?
- **Scheduling.** Where do jobs, stays or appointments live? Calendar, booking system, whiteboard, notebook?
- **Reminders.** Do customers get reminded? Who does it?
- **Invoicing and payment.** Xero, MYOB, something else? How long until you're paid? How much time goes into chasing?
- **After the job.** Do you ask for reviews? Follow up for the next booking or service?
- **Behind the scenes.** Supplier orders, rosters, compliance records, cleaning schedules.

For each one: "How long does that take you each week?"

## 25 to 35 min: What goes wrong

- "Tell me about the last time something fell through the cracks."
- Missed calls or enquiries in a normal week? What's one worth to you?
- Double bookings, no-shows, forgotten follow-ups, unpaid invoices?
- What do you do at night or on weekends that you wish you didn't?
- "If one admin job disappeared tomorrow, which would you pick?"

## 35 to 42 min: Tools and access

- List every app or account the business uses, and who holds the login.
- Which ones do you pay for? Monthly roughly?
- Who set up the website, the email and the domain? Who owns the logins?
- Internet and phone reception at the business and where the work happens.
- Anything you've tried before that didn't stick, and why?

## 42 to 45 min: Wrap up

- "In your words, what's the one thing that would make the biggest difference?"
- Read back the top two or three pains you heard. Check you got them right.
- Next step: "I'll send you a short write-up in the next few days with what I'd do first, what it would cost to run, and what I'd leave alone."
- Ask about comfort level for a small monthly running cost (for example Make or Zapier fees), so the recommendation fits.

## Niche prompts

### Farm stays and agritourism
- Which booking sites do you use (Airbnb, Booking.com, Stayz, your own system)? Do the calendars sync, or do you update them by hand?
- Guest messages before arrival: directions, check-in codes, what to bring. Typed each time?
- Cleaning and changeover: how does the cleaner know?
- Reviews: do you ask, and when?
- Direct bookings vs platform bookings, and the commission you pay.

### Trades
- Missed calls while on the tools: how many, and what happens to them?
- Quotes: how long to send, how many go quiet, do you chase?
- Job management app (ServiceM8, Tradify, Fergus, simPRO) or paper?
- Photos and compliance certificates: where do they end up?
- Invoices: sent on the day, or batched on the weekend?

### Allied health
- Practice software (Cliniko, Nookal, Halaxy, Power Diary)? What does it already automate?
- No-shows and late cancellations a week. Reminder method now?
- New patient intake forms: paper, PDF, online?
- Health funds, NDIS, DVA, Medicare care plan paperwork: how much time?
- Referrer letters and reports: who writes, who sends?
- Remind yourself: no clinical notes through automation tools.
