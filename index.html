Common questions

Straight answers before you book a chat.

Is FieldBourne another all-in-one CRM?

No. It is a lead-conversion and follow-up system. The focus is catching new enquiries and chasing them toward booked, paid work. It is not trying to replace every tool in your business.

Do I have to leave ServiceM8, Tradify or my accounting software?

No. FieldBourne is built to sit beside what you already use. There is no major migration required to start protecting missed enquiries.

I already have a job-management app. Why add this?

Because most job-management tools shine after the job exists. The leak often happens earlier: the missed call, the unread message, the forgotten follow-up. FieldBourne is the front door that catches that work before it slips away.

Will this work from my phone on site?

Yes. It is designed for tradies who live on their phone between jobs, not for people who want another desktop dashboard.

Is this full of AI hype and complicated automation?

No. The story is practical: capture the enquiry, acknowledge the customer, organise the lead, remind you to follow up, then move to quote and booking. No buzzword pitch.

Is this just a missed-call text-back?

Text-back is useful, and FieldBourne can acknowledge a customer fast. The difference is what happens next: organised lead cards, follow-up reminders, quote links, booking, invoicing and review requests. Not a one-off SMS that still leaves the job chasing in your head.

Do you publish pricing on the site?

No. Pricing depends on setup and how your channels work. You get a clear quote on the free chat: implementation plus a flat monthly subscription.
