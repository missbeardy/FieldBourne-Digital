# Client onboarding checklist

Copy this folder to `clients/<client-name>/` for each new client. That copy is gitignored, so client details stay out of git.

## Before work starts

- [ ] Signed quote or proposal, with GST shown
- [ ] Deposit paid
- [ ] Business name, ABN and main contact recorded
- [ ] Hosting tier agreed: client-owned builder, static site hosted by FieldBourne, or managed WordPress (VentraIP)
- [ ] Care plan agreed, or declined in writing

## Domain and DNS

- [ ] Domain is registered in the client's name, not FieldBourne's
- [ ] Client has their own login to the domain registrar
- [ ] Client has their own login to DNS (if separate from the registrar)
- [ ] FieldBourne added as a user or delegate, not as the owner

## Access

- [ ] Google Business Profile: FieldBourne added as a manager, client stays owner
- [ ] Analytics or Search Console: client owns, FieldBourne added as a user
- [ ] Email provider: confirm who manages it and leave it alone unless it is in scope
- [ ] Passwords shared through a password manager or in person, never by email or text

## Content

- [ ] Logo files
- [ ] Brand colours (or agree on them)
- [ ] Photos of real work, with permission to use them
- [ ] Services, prices (if shown), service area and towns
- [ ] Opening hours and how to book
- [ ] Two or three reviews they are happy to show

## Compliance

- [ ] Privacy policy covers any form on the site
- [ ] Contact form sends to an address the client checks
- [ ] Any licence numbers (QBCC, AHPRA, electrical) are shown correctly

## Kick-off

- [ ] Milestones and dates agreed
- [ ] How and when the client gives feedback
- [ ] Date for the handover meeting booked
